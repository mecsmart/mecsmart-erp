Place your application icon here as `icon.ico`.

Requirements:
  - Windows: 256×256 px multi-resolution .ico
  - Recommended sizes embedded: 16, 32, 48, 64, 128, 256

Quick way to generate one:
  1. Export your MecSmart logo as a 1024×1024 PNG (transparent background).
  2. Convert at https://icoconvert.com or with ImageMagick:
        magick logo.png -define icon:auto-resize=16,32,48,64,128,256 icon.ico
  3. Drop the resulting `icon.ico` into this directory.

Without an icon the installer falls back to the default Electron icon — the
app still works, it just looks generic.
